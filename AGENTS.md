# Prototype Instructions

Run the local server yourself and open the preview in the browser available to this environment. Do not give the user server-start instructions when you can run it.

Before making substantial visual changes, use the Product Design plugin's `get-context` skill when the visual source is unclear or no longer matches the current goal. When the user gives durable prototype-specific design feedback, preferences, or decisions, record them in `AGENTS.md`.

When implementing from a selected generated mock, treat that image as the source of truth for layout, component anatomy, density, spacing, color, typography, visible content, and hierarchy.

Build app UI in `src/`. Keep `.openai/hosting.json`, `worker/index.js`, `scripts/prepare-sites-build.mjs`, and `tests/sites-worker.test.mjs` intact so the same local prototype can be handed to Sites. Before a Sites handoff, run `npm run build` and `npm run test:sites`; the build must leave `dist/client/index.html`, `dist/server/index.js`, and `dist/.openai/hosting.json`.

## Durable visual and localization decisions

- Keep the opening product render large (about half the viewport width) and tilted 45 degrees before the scroll transition begins.
- A fresh top-of-page load uses a Lightweight-inspired multi-phase intro: branded loading composition, product close-up, header/frame reveal, horizontal headline mask, then CTA reveal. Skip it for reduced-motion users and non-top hash targets.
- The loading phase is a full-black branded screen with SNAPOD identification, a faint real product cutout that reveals with progress, live tabular percentage, status text, and a full-width fine progress rule. Progress remains driven by critical image, font, and video-metadata readiness; at 100%, the loader wipes upward before the product intro begins.
- On desktop, the opening product begins below the CTA and may be heavily cropped by the bottom edge; mobile keeps the existing lower-half composition.
- The Focus chapter uses a strict split layout: product on the left, copy in a dedicated right column, with no silhouette/text overlap.
- Material callouts in the Layers chapter must sit outside the product silhouette rather than crossing over the render.
- The Layers chapter uses the CAD-derived `public/assets/models/snapod-assembly.glb` in the right-hand product zone. The source is the W1000 × D1000 × H2300 left-opening Solid Edge STEP assembly; keep the Japanese copy on the left and align callouts around the real-time model silhouette.
- The Layers model is scroll-scrubbed and calm: seven semantic assemblies separate from the assembled state, hold briefly, then regroup before the next chapter. It is rendered transparently with Three.js, uses an image fallback while loading or after WebGL failure, and reduced-motion users see a stable exploded state.
- During both chapter handoffs, the assembled booth and the regrouped exploded render must share one center, scale, and motion path so they read as a single object rather than two adjacent objects.
- Reserve independent copy safe zones throughout the sticky product story. On the opening frame, the booth begins below the primary CTA and only rises as the hero copy fades; in `02 / CRAFT`, desktop copy stays in a left column while the booth occupies the right product zone. Do not let product silhouettes pass behind readable copy.
- Public exploded views use the SNAPOD finish system by semantic assembly metadata: sage exterior skins, charcoal structure and hardware, graphite interior textile/floor parts, and neutral translucent glass. Story handoffs use staggered raster/model dissolves and an early zoom-out; avoid simultaneous overlap that creates a ghosted double booth.
- Product renders used over colored or animated sections must have transparent backgrounds; do not reintroduce white studio backdrops.
- The public brand story follows the `lightweight.info` interaction rhythm while retaining SNAPOD copy and assets: one continuous 500vh desktop / 750svh mobile sticky stage, a black-to-pale tone transition, a fixed chapter rail, technical concentric guides, and a shared product coordinate system for close-up, CAD explosion, regrouping, and final product presentation.
- Do not copy or hotlink Lightweight brand assets, fonts, text, or product media. The reference is for layout and motion behavior only; SNAPOD imagery, videos, CAD, copy, and identity remain project-local.
- The fixed header follows the surface beneath it (dark on black scenes, dark ink on pale scenes). On mobile, search expands inside the header and the menu opens as a compact black panel while the product remains visible below.
- Keep the footer content-driven rather than viewport-filling. Preserve the navigation grid and footer metadata, but use one controlled vertical gap between them; do not restore large desktop or mobile `min-height` values that create an empty black screen.
- Keep the exploded-view showcase as the only autoplaying video in the public page. The first wide placement card uses the project-local static `lounge.webp` scene so the placement gallery stays calm and does not compete with the structure film.
- The dark `ENGINEERED QUIET` statement uses the generated project-local `quiet-work-statement-v1.webp` as a static full-bleed lifestyle scene. Preserve the real SNAPOD sage/charcoal/gray-felt product language, the focused worker on the right, and the left-side dark negative space that protects headline and CTA contrast; do not restore a background video.
- The exploded-view showcase appears after the specification strip and before installation scenes, using the project-local 10-second dark-studio exploded-view video with muted inline looping, an accessible play/pause control, and a reduced-motion pause state.
- Customer-facing copy is Japanese-first and written for a Japanese ecommerce landing page: concise CTAs, natural business Japanese, localized units, and conditional notes for performance claims.
- The contact section is a real consultation-flow prototype rather than an email-only strip: collect name, company, business email, inquiry type, expected quantity, optional installation notes, explicit privacy consent, and optional reference attachments. The attachment UI supports click and drag/drop, previews selected names/sizes, removal, up to five image/document/CAD/archive files, and a 10 MB per-file limit. Keep all submission and attachment handling local-only, show the stated 1–2 business-day response expectation, and provide a polished success/reset state.
- Primary CTA buttons use a technical hover/focus treatment: the pill radius resolves into a fine rectangular outline, four crosshair corner marks appear just outside the border, and the whole control lifts only subtly.
- CAD conversion must use the versioned geometry-fingerprint manifest in `scripts/snapod-assembly.manifest.mjs`; positional `childIndexes` are not stable identifiers and must not return.
- The brand GLB carries the seekable `SNAPOD_INSTALL_V1` clip. Keep the door jamb static while the door leaf rotates around its declared Y-axis hinge, and preserve the documented installation sequence when changing the clip.
- Keep the internal CAD workbench as the separate `engineering:*` Vite entry. Never copy source STEP/IGES/BREP files or the OCCT/WASM engineering runtime into the public brand build.
- Do not show the numeric `00—100` progress readout in the lower-right corner of the scroll story; the left chapter rail remains the only visible progress navigation.
- After the sticky product story, desktop product information behaves as a dedicated full-viewport deck: overview/specifications, film, placement scenes, statement, and consultation snap only when nearing a page boundary. Keep the story itself outside this snap container, preserve scroll chaining to the footer, and retain natural document scrolling on mobile.
- The fixed header overlays the information deck; the first overview/specification page must reserve `--header-h` at its top so performance values do not sit under the header at compact desktop widths or on mobile.
- Desktop deck pages for overview, film, spaces, and statement must use the live `100svh` height rather than legacy pixel minimums; otherwise short desktop windows clip film metadata and break the intended full-page transition. The longer contact form remains scrollable by design.
- Header tone must update on the nested `.information-pages` scroll event as well as window scroll, so the fixed navigation changes to its dark treatment over the statement/footer rather than retaining the preceding light-page treatment.
- The scroll rail must contain only the chapter track and chapter controls; do not restore the decorative vertical `PRODUCT STORY` label.
- The product story uses presentation-style scroll turns: wheel/touch settling advances between the hero and the four chapter stops (`0`, `.22`, `.40`, `.59`, `.84`), with the active rail item marked by a short line. Do not trap scrolling after the final SPD01 stop; continuing down must enter the information deck naturally.
- On mobile, keep the whole `02 / CRAFT` text column left-aligned: below 600 px use `left: clamp(96px, 20.5vw, 152px)` with a 26 px right inset; from 600–760 px, use a fixed 152 px left gutter to match `03 / STRUCTURE` on compact desktop windows. Do not independently center or indent its explanatory copy; its eyebrow, title, and body must share one start edge and stay clear of the chapter rail.
